### MedOrd

An Android platform that connects medicine agencies and medical stores. Stock, orders, billing and delivery are tracked in one workflow instead of phone calls and paper ledgers. Orders are tracked through to OTP-verified delivery, with PDF invoicing and automated monthly reports.

`Kotlin` `Jetpack Compose` `Material 3` `MVVM` `Firebase` `Google Maps SDK` `Razorpay`

### ABM EduX

An Android learning platform that brings courses, live classes, tests, assignments, payments and analytics into one app, with separate student and admin experiences. Live classes run on Agora RTC with local recording, and Razorpay payments are verified server-side through Cloud Functions.

`Kotlin` `Jetpack Compose` `Media3 / ExoPlayer` `Firebase` `Agora RTC` `Razorpay`

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-0891B2?style=for-the-badge)](https://lokeshkumar-14.github.io/abm-edux-info/) [![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-6D28D9?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/LokeshKumar-14/abm-edux-info)

### DeskBuddy

A smart display for the Elecrow CrowPanel 7.0" (ESP32-S3, 800×480) with a glass-style touch interface. It shows a clock, weather, calendar, to-do list, SD-card photo gallery and timer, and is controlled from a phone browser with no extra server. Built with LVGL and LovyanGFX.

`C/C++` `Arduino` `ESP32-S3` `LVGL` `LovyanGFX`

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-0891B2?style=for-the-badge)](https://lokeshkumar-14.github.io/Desk-Buddy/web/) [![Source Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/Source_Code-6D28D9?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/LokeshKumar-14/Desk-Buddy)
